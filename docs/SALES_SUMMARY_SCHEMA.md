# Sales Summary Schema

The AI summary should be complete enough to paste into a CRM without additional manual notes.

## Meeting
- Meeting title
- Date/time
- Duration
- Meeting type
- Company
- Industry
- Location / market
- Participants
- Participant roles
- Decision maker
- Relationship to account

## Customer Context
- Current situation
- Business model
- Current marketing/sales setup
- Existing tools/platforms
- Current provider/agency
- Previous attempts
- Desired outcome

## Needs & Pain
- Stated needs
- Implied needs
- Business pain points
- Operational pain points
- Marketing pain points
- Customer impact
- Priority level

## Commercial
- Budget
- Pricing discussed
- Price sensitivity
- Scope requested
- Procurement/payment constraints
- Commercial objections
- Alternatives/competitors

## Qualification
- Problem severity
- Urgency
- Authority
- Budget
- Need
- Timeline
- Buying stage
- Confidence / evidence

## Conversation
- Executive summary
- Key discussion points
- Customer statements
- Questions asked by customer
- Questions asked by salesperson
- Objections
- Responses given
- Agreements
- Disagreements
- Open questions

## Next Steps
- Customer action
- Salesperson action
- Other stakeholder action
- Deadline
- Next meeting
- Follow-up channel
- Recommended follow-up message

## CRM Output
The final output should include:
1. A concise one-paragraph CRM summary.
2. A detailed structured summary.
3. A chronological conversation summary.
4. Explicit objections and how they were handled.
5. Buying signals and risk signals, each backed by transcript evidence.
6. Clear next actions with owner and deadline.
7. Unknown fields marked "Not mentioned" rather than guessed.
